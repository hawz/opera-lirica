descrizione: "Located at the corner of Via De Magistris and Viale Trento, the Teatro Massimo in Cagliari is today run by CeDAC and mainly hosts the city's drama and dance seasons, alongside concerts and live music programming. Distinct from the better-known Teatro Massimo in Palermo, with which it shares only its name, it is an important venue for live performance in Sardinia's capital. The city's opera season, by contrast, typically takes place at the nearby Teatro Lirico."

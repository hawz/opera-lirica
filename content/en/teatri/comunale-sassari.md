descrizione: "Opened in 2012 in the Cappuccini district, the Teatro Comunale is today Sassari's largest hall, seating over thirteen hundred across several levels. It hosts the city's opera and symphonic season alongside drama and major concerts, in a modern building designed for large audiences. It is the contemporary reference point for live music in northern Sardinia."

descrizione: "Born in the early eighteenth century as a private theatre for the princes of Carignano, it was rebuilt by Benedetto Alfieri and reopened in 1753; a fire destroyed it in 1786, though the reconstruction preserved Alfieri's original layout. Passed to the city of Turin in 1870, it has been the historic home of the Teatro Stabile di Torino since 1977. Its elegant horseshoe-shaped hall makes it one of the best-preserved Italian-style theatres in Piedmont, today devoted mainly to spoken drama."

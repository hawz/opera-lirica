---
nome: "Teatro Verdi"
città: "Sassari"
indirizzo: "Corso Vittorio Emanuele 35, 07100 Sassari"
descrizione: "Opened in 1884 inside the Palazzo di Città, the Teatro Verdi is Sassari's historic theatre, its hall modeled on the Teatro Carignano in Turin. Declared a national monument, it carried the city's operatic tradition for more than a century before programming expanded to the more modern Teatro Comunale. It remains a place of great symbolic value for the cultural life of Sassari."
